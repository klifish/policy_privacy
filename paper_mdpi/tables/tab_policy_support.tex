    Publication moratorium (MOR) & Committed & The deadline is bound into the policy as an accepted obligation; publication conduct is not observable at request time. \\
    Negation & Not exposed & The interpreter evaluates NOT, but the DUO~V3 compiler rejects it: membership witnesses prove inclusion only. \\
    Consent withdrawal and policy evolution & On-chain & A successor policy supersedes its predecessor, a policy can be revoked, and a resource can be rebound; earlier grants remain auditable but are no longer current. \\
