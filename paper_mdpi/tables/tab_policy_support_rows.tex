Unrestricted use (NRES) & In-circuit & Compiled as a standalone unconditional policy; it cannot be combined with another DUO term. \\
Categorical data-use terms combined with AND/OR (13 non-procedural DUO attributes, programs up to $64$ tokens) & In-circuit & The committed program and its length are evaluated by the interpreter; each term may appear at most once. \\
Disease, study-design, ancestry, geography, and clinical-term scopes (DS, RS, POA, GS, CC) & In-circuit & Merkle membership of the requested code in a provider-committed whitelist whose root is bound in the parameter commitment. \\
User-, project-, and institution-specific restrictions (US, PS, IS) & In-circuit & Membership of the holder pseudonym in a provider-committed allowlist for the scope. \\
Profile-backed terms (GRU, HMB, NMDS, GSO, NPUNCU, NPOA, NPU, NCU) & In-circuit & EdDSA-Poseidon credential over policy commitment, pseudonym, resource, claims, validity interval, and identifier; the attributes are derived from the signed claims, but subsequent conformity with a declared purpose is not established. \\
Policy validity window & In-circuit + on-chain & The committed window is checked against the public request epoch, which the ACM requires to equal the current hour. \\
Time-limited use (TS) & On-chain & The committed duration starts a per-requester window on the first grant; later requests outside it are denied. TS may appear only conjunctively. \\
Publication moratorium (MOR) & Committed & The deadline is bound into the policy as an accepted obligation; publication conduct is not observable at request time. MOR may appear only conjunctively. \\
Negation & Not exposed & The interpreter evaluates NOT, but the DUO~V3 compiler rejects it: membership witnesses prove inclusion only. \\
Prospective consent withdrawal and policy evolution & On-chain & A successor policy supersedes its predecessor, a policy can be revoked, and a resource can be rebound for future decisions; this does not retract data already released or enforce downstream cessation or deletion. \\
Institutional or ethics approval (IRB) & Not provided & Documentary approval and committee review remain an out-of-circuit authorization step. \\
Downstream purpose limitation, post-access duties (PUB, COL, RTN), delegation, credential revocation & Not provided & Carried by external governance, the data access agreement, and the issuer lifecycle, outside the proved statement. \\
