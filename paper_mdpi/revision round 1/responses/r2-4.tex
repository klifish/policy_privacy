\begin{reviewresponse}{4}
	The gaps were caused by forcing every float to its source position (\texttt{[H]}), which pushes a large table to the next page and leaves the preceding page unfilled.
	All tables and figures now use the standard \texttt{[htbp]} placement, so LaTeX fills each page and floats a large table only when it does not fit.
	We checked the recompiled manuscript page by page: no page other than the last ends with unused space.
	Two of the three tables mentioned were also restructured in the revision (the corpus table now lists the revised corpus; the cost table gained a companion scaling table), which further reduced their height.

\end{reviewresponse}
