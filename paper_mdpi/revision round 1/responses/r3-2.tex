\begin{reviewcomment}{2}
	The current proof statement leaves important soundness gaps.
	The paper acknowledges that the program length is not bound into the policy commitment and that the proof is not bound to the requester, resource, or session context.
	These are not minor implementation details: they directly affect the correctness and replay-resistance of the authorization workflow.
	These issues should be fixed in the design and experimentally re-evaluated, rather than mainly left to future work.
\end{reviewcomment}

\begin{reviewresponse}{2}
	Both gaps are now closed in the design and the implementation, and the evaluation was repeated on the revised system.
	The policy commitment binds the program length ($88$ additional constraints, no public input).
	The proof carries a context digest over requester, resource, and nonce, which the contract recomputes.
	This digest adds $605$ constraints, one public input, and $64{,}022$ gas per access, and a replayed proof with fresh parameters is rejected.
	All results in the revised manuscript come from a new run of $1{,}077$ policies and $1{,}773$ Polygon Amoy executions, plus a new $1{,}119$-execution unpadded ablation.
	The cost profile reflects the revised circuit ($81{,}740$ constraints).
