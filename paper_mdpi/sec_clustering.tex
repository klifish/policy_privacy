\subsection{Unsupervised Clustering: A Label-Free Cross-Check}
\label{sec:eval-clustering}
The clustering attacker stated in Section~\ref{sec:eval-method} tests whether the on-chain observables group by a hidden label without access to that label.
Table~\ref{tab:clustering-summary} summarizes the clustering results across the three privacy objectives.

For PCH and SPP, ARI remains within $\pm 0.006$, indicating little agreement between the clusters and the hidden labels, and NMI is at most $0.044$.
Across the twelve permutation tests for these targets, only the NMI for \texttt{nestingDepth} is nominally significant ($0.0072$, $p_{\mathrm{NMI}}=0.04$).
Its ARI is not statistically significant ($p_{\mathrm{ARI}}=0.20$).
The isolated NMI result should be interpreted cautiously in the context of twelve tests at the $5\%$ significance level.
In the separate supervised evaluation, all \texttt{nestingDepth} tasks are classified as PASS under the leakage criterion (Section~\ref{sec:eval-spp}).

For PI, the mean ARI and NMI across the $30$ policy pairs are $-0.0020$ and $0.0242$, respectively.
These clustering findings complement the supervised results on the same dataset.
