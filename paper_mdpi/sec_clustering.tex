\subsection{Unsupervised Clustering: A Label-Free Cross-Check}
\label{sec:eval-clustering}

The supervised attacks (Sections~\ref{sec:eval-pch}--\ref{sec:eval-spp}) assume an adversary who can train on labeled executions.
A weaker, label-free attacker asks whether the on-chain gas features \emph{cluster} along the hidden property with no supervision at all.

\input{tables/tab_clustering_summary.tex}

Table~\ref{tab:clustering-summary} consolidates this attack across all three objectives: K-Means recovers essentially nothing, with ARI within $\pm 0.003$ and NMI below $0.03$ for every DUO, PI, and SPP target except one.
NMI values are interpreted against the null simulation in Section~\ref{sec:eval-nmi-sensitivity}: the slightly higher ICD-10 NMI ($0.0281$) lies within the range of a signal-free ten-class clustering with the same chapter sizes ($p=0.15$), and permuting the chapter labels against the observed clusters gives the same reading (debiased NMI $0.0044$, $p=0.08$).
The exception is the SPP \texttt{nestingDepth} axis, whose ARI of $0.007$ is tiny in magnitude but permutation-significant ($p_{\mathrm{ARI}}=0.005$). It mirrors the \texttt{medium}/\texttt{deep} failure reported in Section~\ref{sec:eval-spp}: clustering the same executions on the residual gas (access gas minus the padding target) returns the axis to ARI $-0.0004$ ($p=0.60$), and no other axis exceeds ARI $0.003$ on the residual (\texttt{paramType} reaches $0.0026$, $p=0.04$). The grouping therefore follows the per-policy random draws of the padding.
With that qualification, the unsupervised attacker agrees with the supervised results as a second, label-free attack family on the same dataset (Figure~\ref{fig:clust-summary}).

\noindent\textbf{Removing sample-size confounds.} The PI clustering inputs are rebalanced into $30$-vs-$30$ contrasts and permutation-debiased as described in Section~\ref{sec:eval-method}. Over the $127$ balanced pairs, the mean ARI is $0.002$, the mean debiased NMI $0.001$, and $6$ pairs ($5\%$) reach nominal significance, the fraction expected under the null at $\alpha=0.05$. The conclusion matches the supervised PI result (Section~\ref{sec:eval-pi}), and the ablation (Section~\ref{sec:eval-ablation}) confirms the clustering device itself is live: on the unpadded transcript the same balanced pairs cluster with a mean debiased NMI $0.67$ higher, and the signal disappears once padding is applied.
