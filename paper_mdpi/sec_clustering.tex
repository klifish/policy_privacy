\subsection{Unsupervised Clustering: A Label-Free Cross-Check}
\label{sec:eval-clustering}

Table~\ref{tab:clustering-summary} summarizes the clustering results for the three privacy objectives using the method described in Section~\ref{sec:eval-method}.
ARI and NMI remain close to zero across the evaluated targets, indicating little agreement between the clusters and the hidden labels.
The SPP \texttt{nestingDepth} axis yields a small ARI of $0.0071$, which is statistically significant under the label-permutation test ($p_{\mathrm{ARI}}=0.005$).
It mirrors the \texttt{medium}/\texttt{deep} failure reported in Section~\ref{sec:eval-spp}: clustering the same executions on the residual gas (access gas minus the padding target) returns the axis to ARI $-0.0004$ ($p=0.60$), and no other axis exceeds ARI $0.003$ on the residual (\texttt{paramType} reaches $0.0026$, $p=0.04$). 
The grouping therefore follows the per-policy random draws of the padding.
With that qualification, the unsupervised attacker agrees with the supervised results as a second, label-free attack family on the same dataset (Figure~\ref{fig:clust-summary}).
