\subsection{Unsupervised Clustering: A Label-Free Cross-Check}
\label{sec:eval-clustering}
The clustering attacker stated in Section~\ref{sec:eval-method} tests whether the on-chain observables group by a hidden label without access to that label.
Table~\ref{tab:clustering-summary} summarizes the clustering results across the three privacy objectives.

ARI stays within $\pm 0.006$ for every PCH, PI, and SPP target, indicating little agreement between the clusters and the hidden labels, and the PCH and SPP NMI values are at most $0.044$.
Of the twelve permutation tests for the six PCH and SPP targets, one is nominally significant: the NMI for \texttt{nestingDepth} ($0.0072$, $p_{\mathrm{NMI}}=0.04$), whose ARI is not ($p_{\mathrm{ARI}}=0.20$).
With twelve tests at the $5\%$ level, one such result is consistent with chance, and all six supervised \texttt{nestingDepth} tasks pass (Section~\ref{sec:eval-spp}).
For PI, $1$ of the $30$ pairs ($3\%$) has $p_{\mathrm{NMI}}<0.05$, consistent with the nominal $5\%$ level.
These findings complement the supervised results on the same dataset.
