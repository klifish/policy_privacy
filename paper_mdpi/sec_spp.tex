\subsection{Structure \& Parameter Privacy (SPP)}
\label{sec:eval-spp}

\noindent\textbf{Property.} SPP requires that neither the \emph{shape} of a policy (logical structure, nesting depth, predicate coverage) nor the \emph{size} of its parameters (whitelist/set sizes) leaks to the observer. The supervised attack organizes the test along four annotation axes---logical structure type (\texttt{structureType}), predicate count (\texttt{predicateCombo}), parameter size (\texttt{paramType}), and nesting depth (\texttt{nestingDepth})---and attacks each pair of buckets within an axis.
\texttt{structureType} has five buckets: \texttt{trivial} (a single predicate with no boolean connective, e.g.\
policy logic \texttt{"GR"}), \texttt{and\_only}, \texttt{or\_only}, \texttt{and\_or\_mixed},
and \texttt{negation\_heavy}. \texttt{predicateCombo} buckets policies by predicate-namespace count into \texttt{none} ($0$), \texttt{single} ($1$), \texttt{few} ($2$--$3$), and \texttt{many} ($\ge 4$); \texttt{paramType} buckets by whitelist size into \texttt{none} (no whitelist), \texttt{small} ($1$--$4$), \texttt{medium} ($5$--$16$), and \texttt{large} ($\ge 17$); \texttt{nestingDepth} buckets by structural nesting into \texttt{flat}, \texttt{shallow}, \texttt{medium}, and \texttt{deep}. The per-bucket policy and execution counts are listed in Table~\ref{tab:corpus}. Contrasting every within-axis bucket pair gives $10+6+6+6=28$ contrasts.

\noindent\textbf{Supervised attack.} Table~\ref{tab:spp-contrasts} reports all 28 pairwise contrasts over 30 policy-disjoint seeds, with the per-contrast AUC taken from the higher-scoring of the two classifiers following the convention of Section~\ref{sec:eval-method}.
Most contrasts sit at chance, with $\mathrm{AUC}_{\text{mean}}\in[0.48,0.54]$. Four exceed the $0.55$ line---all separating the tiny \texttt{none}-predicate or \texttt{none}-parameter class from a populated one: \texttt{none}/\texttt{few} ($0.629$), \texttt{none}/\texttt{many} ($0.617$), \texttt{paramType}~\texttt{none}/\texttt{medium} ($0.612$), and \texttt{none}/\texttt{single} ($0.582$). These buckets hold very few minority-class executions, so an individual split can look separable; but across the 30 seeds their significant-seed fractions stay at or below $20\%$---and no contrast exceeds $23\%$ overall---far short of the $50\%$ the criterion requires. All 28 therefore pass and SPP passes overall. Reporting AUC rather than an accuracy-style score already discounts majority-class prediction (Section~\ref{sec:eval-method}); the residual elevation on the \texttt{none} contrasts reflects their small, low-power minority class rather than a stable structural or parameter-size channel.
Section~\ref{sec:eval-ablation} provides the corresponding positive control: when gas padding is removed, the same SPP attacker recovers structural and parameter-size signal, and that signal collapses under the full normalization.
