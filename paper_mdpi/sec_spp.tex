\subsection{Structure and Parameter Privacy (SPP)}
\label{sec:eval-spp}

\textbf{Objective.} SPP requires that neither the \emph{shape} of a policy (logical structure, nesting depth, predicate coverage) nor the \emph{size} of its parameters (whitelist/set sizes) leaks to the observer. We organize the test along four annotation axes---logical structure type (\texttt{structureType}), predicate count (\texttt{predicateCombo}), parameter size (\texttt{paramType}), and nesting depth (\texttt{nestingDepth})---and test each pair of buckets within an axis.

\texttt{structureType} has four buckets: \texttt{trivial} (a single predicate with no Boolean connective, e.g.\ policy logic \texttt{"DS"}), \texttt{and\_only}, \texttt{or\_only}, and \texttt{and\_or\_mixed}. \texttt{predicateCombo} buckets policies by predicate-namespace count into \texttt{single} ($1$), \texttt{few} ($2$--$3$), and \texttt{many} ($\ge 4$); \texttt{paramType} buckets by whitelist size into \texttt{none} (no whitelist: the policy is decided by profile-backed terms only), \texttt{small} ($1$--$4$), \texttt{medium} ($5$--$16$), and \texttt{large} ($\ge 17$); \texttt{nestingDepth} buckets by the maximum nesting of the normalized expression into \texttt{flat} ($\le 1$), \texttt{shallow} ($2$), \texttt{medium} ($3$), and \texttt{deep} ($\ge 4$). The generator draws the whitelist size independently of the logical form, so the four axes are not functions of one another (Section~\ref{sec:eval-dataset}); the per-bucket policy and execution counts are listed in Table~\ref{tab:corpus}.

\textbf{Supervised attack.} Table~\ref{tab:spp-contrasts} reports all 21 pairwise contrasts over 30 policy-disjoint seeds, with the per-contrast AUC taken from the higher-scoring of the two classifiers following the convention of Section~\ref{sec:eval-method}.
Most contrasts sit at chance, with mean AUC between $0.47$ and $0.54$. Three exceed the $0.55$ line without reproducing: \texttt{paramType} \texttt{none}/\texttt{small} ($0.571$, $40\%$ significant seeds), \texttt{none}/\texttt{large} ($0.568$, $33\%$), and \texttt{nestingDepth} \texttt{flat}/\texttt{medium} ($0.555$, $27\%$); the two \texttt{none} contrasts separate the profile-only policies, whose $147$ policies form the smallest minority class of the axis. One contrast, \texttt{nestingDepth} \texttt{medium}/\texttt{deep}, meets both conditions of the criterion (mean AUC $0.563$, $53\%$ significant seeds) and is reported as FAIL.

Its separability has the same origin as the PCH ch5 case (Section~\ref{sec:eval-pch}).
The single-feature rank AUC of the access gas for this contrast equals that of the padding target alone ($0.440$ for both, i.e.\ deep policies drew lower targets) and the residual after subtracting the target scores $0.467$; over all 21 contrasts the gas and target AUCs agree to three decimals, and the residual AUC stays within $0.47$--$0.56$.
Rerunning the protocol with the residual in place of the access gas returns the contrast to PASS (logistic regression $0.530$ with $13\%$ significant seeds; random forest $0.544$ with $33\%$), while the target alone reproduces the FAIL ($0.568$, $57\%$).
What remains above chance on this contrast is the per-policy random draw that the padding itself introduces, not a stable structural channel.
Section~\ref{sec:eval-ablation} provides the corresponding positive control: when gas padding is removed, the same SPP attacker separates structural and parameter-size buckets with AUCs up to $0.90$, and that signal collapses once padding is applied.
