\subsection{Policy Content Hiding (PCH)}
\label{sec:eval-pch}

\paragraph{Property.} PCH requires that the on-chain observer cannot recover the
\emph{content} of a policy---its DUO data-use terms or the ICD-10 disease codes it
references---better than chance.

\paragraph{Scope of the labels.} We do not attack the full Data Use Ontology and its
linked ontologies. The complete DUO has 23 data-use terms, and ICD-10 has tens of
thousands of individual codes; most never appear in our generated policies, and those
that do are too rare to support a statistically powered one-vs-rest attack. We therefore
evaluate the six DUO terms that occur with enough support in the dataset (GR, DS, RS,
POA, GS, CC; smallest class $n{=}30$) and aggregate disease codes to the ten ICD-10
chapters. This is the right granularity for the privacy question---the observer cares
whether the \emph{category} of permitted use or disease leaks, not which leaf code is
used---and it is also a conservative choice: attacks on coarser, better-supported labels
are \emph{easier} than on rare leaf terms, so passing here does not understate the leak.
The defenses (commitment-only interface, gas padding) are content-agnostic and apply
identically to the terms we do not enumerate.

\paragraph{Supervised attack.} We treat each of the six DUO terms (GR, DS, RS, POA, GS,
CC) as a one-vs-rest binary recovery task and run the multi-seed pipeline of
Section~\ref{sec:eval-method}. Table~\ref{tab:pch-terms} reports the result: every term
has $\mathrm{AUC}_{\text{mean}}\in[0.44,0.54]$ with a significant-seed fraction at or
below $10\%$, so all six pass and PCH passes overall. The same pipeline applied to the
ten ICD-10 chapters (one-vs-rest over the ICD-bearing rows only; Table~\ref{tab:pch-icd10})
passes all ten. The two highest-AUC chapters are the only ones worth scrutinizing---ch6
at $\mathrm{AUC}_{\text{mean}}=0.607\pm0.066$ and ch4 at $0.578\pm0.068$---yet neither is
permutation-significant (significant-seed fraction $0.37$ and $0.33$; mean $p=0.148$ and
$0.210$), so under the dual criterion both are noise rather than a channel. We report
these boundary cases rather than suppress them.

\paragraph{Positive control.} On the synthetic \texttt{gas\,>\,median} label the same
pipeline reaches AUC $0.994\pm0.002$ and $F_1\approx 0.95$, confirming the chance-level
AUC on real DUO/ICD-10 labels reflects the absence of signal, not a broken attack.

\paragraph{Clustering attack.} Unsupervised K-Means over the gas features, clustering all
executions globally, recovers neither label: DUO terms give ARI $0.0002$ / NMI $0.0036$
(all $2005$ executions), and the ten ICD-10 chapters give ARI $-0.0010$ / NMI $0.0181$
(all $920$ ICD-bearing executions); see Table~\ref{tab:clustering-summary}. Both are
indistinguishable from random partitions, so policy content is hidden under the
unsupervised attacker as well as the supervised one.
