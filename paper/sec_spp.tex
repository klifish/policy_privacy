\subsection{Structure \& Parameter Privacy (SPP)}
\label{sec:eval-spp}

\paragraph{Property.} SPP requires that neither the \emph{shape} of a policy (logical
structure, nesting depth, predicate coverage) nor the \emph{magnitude} of its parameters
(whitelist/set sizes) leaks to the observer. The supervised attack organizes the test
along three annotation axes---logical structure type, predicate count, and set-size
magnitude---and attacks each pair of buckets within an axis; the unsupervised attack
additionally targets nesting depth (Table~\ref{tab:clustering-summary}).

\paragraph{Supervised attack.} Table~\ref{tab:spp-contrasts} reports all 12 pairwise
contrasts over 30 policy-disjoint seeds, restricted to buckets with adequate support
(the empty-predicate and empty-parameter \texttt{none} buckets hold only nine policies
each, too few for a stable policy-disjoint split, and are excluded). AUC is the calibrated
random-forest score, since logistic regression over-separates on the near-constant SPP
features. Every retained contrast has $\mathrm{AUC}_{\text{mean}}\in[0.43,0.52]$ and a
significant-seed fraction at or below $27\%$, so all 12 pass and SPP passes overall. We
gate on AUC and the permutation test rather than on raw advantage: single-split
logistic-regression advantage runs large ($0.15$--$0.30$) on these imbalanced contrasts,
but this is the majority-class artifact described in Section~\ref{sec:eval-method}---a
near-constant predictor scoring high advantage without genuine separation---which the
AUC-plus-permutation criterion correctly reads as chance.

\paragraph{Positive control.} The synthetic \texttt{gas\,>\,median} control reaches AUC
$1.000\pm0.001$ and $F_1=1.00$ through the same pipeline, validating that the
chance-level scores on real structure/parameter labels reflect the absence of signal.

\paragraph{Clustering attack.} Unsupervised K-Means over the gas features recovers none
of the four structural targets (Table~\ref{tab:clustering-summary}): \texttt{logicStructure}
ARI $-0.0007$/NMI $0.0022$, \texttt{nestingDepth} $-0.0002$/$0.0018$,
\texttt{predicateCount} $-0.0008$/$0.0015$, and \texttt{setSize} $-0.0013$/$0.0019$---all
indistinguishable from random partitions. Structure and parameters are hidden under both
the supervised and the unsupervised attacker.
