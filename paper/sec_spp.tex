\subsection{Structure \& Parameter Privacy (SPP)}
\label{sec:eval-spp}

\paragraph{Property} SPP requires that neither the \emph{shape} of a policy (logical structure, nesting depth, predicate coverage) nor the \emph{size} of its parameters (whitelist/set sizes) leaks to the observer. The supervised attack organizes the test along four annotation axes---logical structure type (\texttt{structureType}), predicate count (\texttt{predicateCombo}), parameter size (\texttt{paramType}), and nesting depth (\texttt{nestingDepth})---and attacks each pair of buckets within an axis.
\texttt{structureType} has five buckets: \texttt{trivial} (a single predicate with no boolean connective, e.g.\
policy logic \texttt{"GR"}), \texttt{and\_only}, \texttt{or\_only}, \texttt{and\_or\_mixed},
and \texttt{negation\_heavy}. \texttt{predicateCombo} buckets policies by predicate-namespace count into \texttt{none} ($0$), \texttt{single} ($1$), \texttt{few} ($2$--$3$), and \texttt{many} ($\ge 4$); \texttt{paramType} buckets by whitelist size into \texttt{none} (no whitelist), \texttt{small} ($1$--$4$), \texttt{medium} ($5$--$16$), and \texttt{large} ($\ge 17$); \texttt{nestingDepth} buckets by structural nesting into \texttt{flat}, \texttt{shallow}, \texttt{medium}, and \texttt{deep}. Contrasting every within-axis bucket pair gives $10+6+6+6=28$ contrasts.

\paragraph{Supervised attack} Table~\ref{tab:spp-contrasts} reports all 28 pairwise contrasts over 30 policy-disjoint seeds, with the per-contrast AUC taken from the higher-scoring of the two classifiers following the convention of Section~\ref{sec:eval-method}.
Every contrast has $\mathrm{AUC}_{\text{mean}}\in[0.421,0.530]$ and a significant-seed fraction at or below $20\%$, so all 28 pass the AUC-plus-permutation criterion and SPP passes overall. Reporting AUC keeps the imbalanced contrasts (e.g.\ the small \texttt{none} buckets) from registering apparent gains that are only majority-class prediction, as noted in Section~\ref{sec:eval-method}.
Section~\ref{sec:eval-ablation} provides the corresponding positive control: when gas padding is removed, the same SPP attacker recovers structural and parameter-size signal, and that signal collapses under the full normalization.
